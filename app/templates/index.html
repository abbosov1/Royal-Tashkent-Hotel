Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes, most room categories include free cancellation before check-in.

Do you offer early check-in?

Subject to room availability. Contact concierge in advance for priority handling.

Is airport transfer available?

Yes, we provide transfer from and to Tashkent airport on request.

Can I reschedule dates after booking?

Yes. Open your Dashboard and use the reschedule option for your booking.

Do you have family-friendly rooms?

Yes, we have spacious categories suitable for families, with additional bedding options upon request.

Is breakfast included?

Some room plans include breakfast. You can verify this in room details before completing payment.
